Guitar samples in this folder
=============================

Source: "Electric Guitar FSBS Jazz" (bridge pickup) sound bank by the FreePats project
        https://freepats.zenvoid.org/ElectricGuitar/clean-electric-guitar.html
        https://github.com/freepats/electric-guitar-FSBS-jazz

License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
         https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
         -> free for any use, including commercial, no attribution required
            (credited here anyway as a courtesy).

Changes made for this app: 35 of the bank's samples (round-robin take 01 for every zone used,
plus take 02 for the most-repeated notes, normal and soft layers) were trimmed to 3-5 s with a fade-out, mixed to mono, resampled to 32 kHz,
level-matched, and saved as 16-bit WAV. The app plays them as a small sampler.
