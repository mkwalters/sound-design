01

Warm‑up

Sound in 30 seconds

Sound is a wiggle in the air. Three things describe it.

  • Height → loudness
  • Speed → pitch
  • Shape → tone

Try Switch Sine → Square. It's the same note with a buzzier tone, because square waves carry extra harmonics.

The map

Pedals sit between guitar and amp. Each changes one thing about the sound.

03

Shape

Overdrive, distortion & fuzz

Push the signal into a ceiling. The flattened peaks are the grit.

  • Overdrive gentle, bluesy
  • Distortion harder, tighter
  • Fuzz nearly square, buzzy

Try Sweep Drive up slowly. Flatter peaks mean more harmonics, which means more grit.

04

Dynamics

The compressor

An automatic volume knob that turns loud notes down.

  • Threshold where squashing starts
  • Ratio how hard it squashes
  • Result punchy, even, sustained

Try Stomp it on and off. The quiet blips catch up to the loud ones.

05

Frequency

Filters & the wah

Keep some frequencies, cut others. A wah sweeps a peak with your foot.

  • Tone knob a low‑pass filter
  • Wah a moving peak, like saying “oo‑aah”
  • Auto‑wah follows your picking

Try Drag the treadle up and down. Then pick Envelope with the Lead lick.

06

Modulation

Tremolo & vibrato

An LFO is a slow, silent wave that turns a knob for you, over and over.

  • Tremolo LFO → volume
  • Vibrato LFO → pitch
  • Square choppy stutter

Try Flip between Tremolo and Vibrato: same LFO, different target. (Fun fact: Fender swapped these two names for decades.)

07

Modulation

Chorus & flanger

Mix in a slightly delayed copy, and let an LFO wobble the delay.

  • Chorus 15–30 ms: shimmer, like two players
  • Flanger 1–5 ms + feedback: jet whoosh
  • Comb overlap cancels some frequencies

Try Click Chorus, then Flanger. Watch the comb's teeth slide.

08

Modulation

The phaser

Shift the timing of some frequencies, mix with the original, and a few notches sweep.

  • vs. flanger fewer, softer notches
  • Stages every 2 = 1 notch
  • Sound smooth, vocal swirl

Try Switch 2 → 8 stages and count the notches.

09

Time

Delay & echo

Plays back what you played, a moment later. Feedback repeats the repeats.

  • < 50 ms doubling
  • ~100 ms slapback
  • 300 ms + echo

Try Step through the presets. Then twist Time while echoes ring.

10

Time

Reverb

Thousands of reflections blurred together: the sound of a room.

  • Early reflections room size
  • Tail the fading wash
  • Spring & plate vintage hardware

Try Room → Hall → Spring. The space grows, then turns boingy.

11

Putting it together

Build a pedalboard

Chain them all, and remember that order matters.

wah → comp → drive → modulation → delay → reverb

Try Load Backwards!: reverb runs into distortion, and it all turns to mush.
